## Full description (max 4000 chars)

Know exactly what you're worth. Net Worth Calculator is a clean, private way to
track everything you own and everything you owe — and watch your net worth grow.

Built for India (₹), it works fully offline on your phone. There's no account
required, no ads, and no analytics. Sign in with Google only if you want your
data mirrored to your own Google Drive.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• See your total net worth at a glance, with month-over-month change
• Break down assets vs liabilities, and where your money sits
• Visualise your asset allocation with a clean donut chart
• Check how much of your wealth is liquid vs locked away
• Follow your net-worth trend over time
• Drill into any category to add, edit, hide, or annotate items
• Track your whole household — tag items to family members and toggle who's
  included in the totals
• Price gold & silver live by weight (grams × today's rate)
• Export everything to CSV whenever you want

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• All your data stays on your device by default
• Optional Google sign-in syncs to a single spreadsheet in YOUR Google Drive —
  the app can only touch the file it created, nothing else
• No servers, no ads, no tracking

BEAUTIFUL & FAST
• A modern, native app built with Jetpack Compose
• Full dark and light themes
• Smooth, animated charts and a thoughtful, clutter-free design

Whether you're building wealth, paying down debt, or just want a clear monthly
snapshot of your finances, Net Worth Calculator keeps it simple, private, and
yours.
