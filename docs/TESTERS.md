# Closed testing — instructions for testers

Thanks for helping test **Net Worth Calculator**! It's a private, offline-first
app to track your assets, liabilities, and net worth (in ₹).

## How to join
1. Tap the **opt-in link** I shared with you (Play Console → Closed testing →
   testers), or accept the email invite.
2. Install **Net Worth Calculator** from Google Play.
3. Use it normally for a few days — the more real-world use, the better.

> New apps need a group of testers to use the app over ~2 weeks before it can go
> public, so just keeping it installed and opening it now and then genuinely
> helps. 🙏

## What to try
- **Add items** — a few assets (cash, stocks, gold, property) and a liability or
  two (loan, credit card). Try **gold by weight** (enter grams).
- **Explore the dashboard** — net worth, assets-vs-liabilities, the allocation
  donut, liquid-vs-locked, and (after using it into a new month) the trend.
- **Household** — add a family member, tag items to them, and toggle who's
  included in the totals.
- **Drill into a category** — edit, hide, add notes.
- **Theme** — switch between dark and light.
- **Export** — try the CSV export from the ⋮ menu.
- **(Optional) Google sync** — tap the **Account** tab → **Sign in**. Your data
  syncs to a private "Net worth data" sheet in your own Google Drive. Sign-in is
  completely optional; the app works fully offline without it.
  - Use the **same Google account you shared with me** (it has to be on the
    approved tester list to sign in while the app is in testing).
  - You may see a **"Google hasn't verified this app"** screen — that's normal
    for an app still in testing. Tap **Advanced → Continue** (it's my app, and it
    only ever touches the one spreadsheet it creates).

## What to look for
- Anything that **crashes, freezes, or looks wrong**
- Numbers that don't add up
- Confusing wording or layout
- Sync issues (if you signed in)

## Sending feedback
Reply to my message / email with: **what you did**, **what happened**, **what
you expected**, and your **phone model + Android version**. Screenshots are gold. 🙌

## Privacy
Your data stays on your device (and, only if you sign in, in your own Google
Drive). Nothing is sent to me or any server. Full policy: see the app's privacy
policy link on its Play Store page.
